      Frequently asked questions

      How are travel times calculated?

      Scheduled services on a reference weekday, 07:00 to 20:00. Walking uses straight lines at 4.5 km/h, with bridges across rivers on the city map. Waiting is half the average headway, capped at 30 min. Transfers add 1.5 min walking and waiting, plus 1 min platform access for trains and the funicular. Train categories are split into lines by terminus. The Pfaffenthal-Kirchberg funicular is added manually because it is absent from the GTFS.

      Are buses included?

      Yes, by default: AVL, RGTR, TICE and CFL buses. Uncheck Bus to use only rail transport. Public transport in Luxembourg is free.

      Where does the data come from?

      GTFS from the Administration des transports publics (ATP), on data.public.lu, CC-BY 4.0. Municipal boundaries, water, parks, rivers and bridges: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Address search: geoportail.lu.

      Is this real-time information?

      No. Times are estimates based on scheduled services. Disruptions and individual walking streets are not included.
